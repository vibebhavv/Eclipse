// the problem

D2C brands are the
perfect target.

You collect customer names, addresses, phone numbers, and payment data at scale — with startup-level security. That gap is expensive.

01 — BREACH RISK

Your Shopify store has gaps you don't know about

Misconfigured admin panels, exposed APIs, weak passwords on team accounts — these are the top 3 entry points attackers use against Indian e-commerce stores. We find them before attackers do.

02 — LEGAL RISK

DPDPA fines can shut you down

India's new data protection law carries fines up to ₹250 crore. Full enforcement hits May 2027. If you collect customer data — and every D2C brand does — you're legally required to protect it.

03 — BRAND RISK

One breach headline undoes years of trust

boAt lost 7.5 million records. The news cycle destroyed their customer trust overnight. A small D2C brand with 10,000 customers losing their data has no PR team to manage the fallout.

04 — THE GAP

Enterprise firms won't serve you at your size

Big cybersecurity firms charge ₹10–20 lakh minimum. You're invisible to them. We built Eclipse specifically for growing D2C brands — real protection at a price that fits your stage.
